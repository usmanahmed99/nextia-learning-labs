# ADR-NNNN: <the decision in a few words>

An architecture decision record (ADR) keeps one decision, why we took it and when to look at it again. Copy this file to `adr-NNNN-short-name.md`.

## Status

Proposed, Accepted, or Replaced by ADR-NNNN. Who decided (the commit that adds the file keeps the date).

## Context

The problem, the requirement it serves (Q1 ... Q8 in requirements.md) and the numbers that matter, each labelled measured, price or assumption.

## Options

Two or three real options. For each: what it costs, what it does well, what it does badly.

## Decision

The option we chose, in one sentence.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now do.

## Revisit when

The measured signal that makes us look at this decision again (for example "the busiest minute uses 70% of the provider quota").
