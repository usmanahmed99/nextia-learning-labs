# Manual refunds and goodwill credits

## When this procedure applies

Refunds for normal returns are approved automatically after inspection. Use this procedure for manual refunds and goodwill credits, which you decide as an agent.

## Approval limits for manual refunds

- Agent: up to 100 dollars.
- Team lead: up to 500 dollars.
- Support lead (Grace): over 500 dollars.

## Goodwill credit

You may give a goodwill store credit of up to 25 dollars without approval, once per customer in 12 months.

## Rules

- Refund only to the original payment method. If that card is closed, Finance pays by Interac e-Transfer.
- Never refund without the item coming back, except damaged items under 50 dollars and warranty claims under the warranty claims procedure. Any other exception needs a team lead.
- For a double charge, confirm both charges in the payment dashboard first. Finance refunds the extra charge within 3 business days.

## Reason codes

Log every manual refund with one reason code: RFD-GOODWILL, RFD-DAMAGE, RFD-LATE, RFD-PRICEADJ or RFD-OTHER.
