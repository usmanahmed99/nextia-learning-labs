# Larkfield warranty policy

## What the warranty covers

Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use. The warranty period starts on the delivery date.

We repair the item, replace it, or refund it. We choose the remedy.

A repaired or replacement item keeps the remaining period of the original warranty; it does not start a new one.

## Warranty periods by product category

| Product category | Examples | Warranty period | Exceptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hand garden tools | spades, rakes, pruners, shears | 2 years | none |
| Garden furniture | chairs, tables, loungers, benches | 2 years | cushions and fabric covers: 1 year |
| Corded power tools | hedge trimmers such as the HT-550, corded lawn mowers | 3 years | blades: not covered |
| Battery power tools | the Larkfield Volt 20 V range | 3 years | batteries and chargers: 1 year |
| Pressure washers | PW-2200, PW-2400 | 2 years | pump: 3 years; nozzles and hoses: 1 year |
| BBQs and grills | the Ember GR-310 | 2 years | stainless-steel burners: 5 years |
| Sheds and greenhouses | wooden and metal sheds, greenhouses | 5 years | glass and polycarbonate panels: 1 year |
| Watering | hoses, hose reels, sprinklers, timers | 1 year | none |
| Small kitchen appliances | kettles, toasters, blenders | 1 year | none |
| Lighting | indoor lamps, outdoor and solar lights | 1 year | none |
| Textiles | parasols, hammocks, outdoor rugs | 1 year | none |
| Larkfield Pro range | any product marked Pro | 5 years | covers trade use |

If a product fits two categories, the longer period applies.

## What is not covered

- Normal wear.
- Misuse, accidents, and repairs or changes made by someone other than Larkfield.
- Rust from leaving tools outside.
- Frost damage, for example a pressure washer stored with water inside below 0 °C.
- Damage from the wrong fuel, voltage or chemicals.
- Commercial or trade use, except for the Larkfield Pro range.
- Consumable parts, such as blades, nozzles, filters and cutting line, unless the table lists them.

## How to make a claim

Contact the help desk with your order number and a photo or short video of the fault.

For power tools and pressure washers, also give the serial number from the label on the product.

Do not send the product back until we ask you to. We send a free return label if we need the product.

## Proof of purchase

Your order number is your proof of purchase. For a gift, the gift receipt or the giver's order number is enough.

## Customers in Quebec

Customers in Quebec also have the legal warranty under Quebec law, in addition to this warranty. See the French document Garantie légale au Québec.

## Safety problems

If a product smokes, sparks, overheats, smells of burning or leaks gas, stop using it and contact us at once. Safety notices win over this policy.
