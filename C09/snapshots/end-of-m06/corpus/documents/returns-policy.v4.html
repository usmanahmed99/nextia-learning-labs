Returns policy

Version 4 · Effective from 2026-11-01 · Owner: Support (Grace)

Who this policy is for

This policy applies to orders delivered in Canada.

The version that applies is the one in force on the date the order was delivered. Orders delivered before 1 November 2026 follow version 3 (30 days).

Return window

You can return an unused item within 45 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.

The 45 days start on the delivery date shown in your account.

Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, with all parts, accessories and manuals.

Opened packaging is accepted if the item is unused.

A used item cannot be returned because you changed your mind. If a used item is faulty, see the warranty policy.

Items that cannot be returned

  • Live plants and seeds.
  • Products cut to length, such as hose or fabric sold by the metre.
  • Gift cards.
  • Personalised items.
  • Opened bags of soil, fertiliser or garden chemicals.

How to start a return

Go to Orders in your account, choose the item and select Return an item. Or contact the help desk.

We email a return label within 1 business day.

Drop the parcel at any post office.

Items over 30 kg: we arrange a pickup instead of a label. The pickup fee is 49 dollars, taken from the refund.

Return shipping costs

You pay return shipping: 12.95 dollars is taken from the refund.

Larkfield Rewards members get a free return label (the 49-dollar pickup fee for items over 30 kg still applies).

Return shipping is free when the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

Refunds

We inspect the item within 3 business days after it reaches us, then approve the refund.

The refund goes to the original payment method. See the refunds and payments policy for how long it takes to arrive.

The original shipping fee is not refunded, unless you return the whole order because of our error.

Exchanges

We do not exchange items directly. Return the item and place a new order.

What changed in this version

  • Return window: 45 days instead of 30 days, for orders delivered on or after 1 November 2026.
  • New: a free return label for Larkfield Rewards members.
