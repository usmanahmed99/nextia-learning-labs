# Trade and business accounts

## Who can open a trade account

Landscapers, contractors and other businesses with a GST/HST number can open a trade account.

## Benefits

- 10% off the Larkfield Pro range.
- Payment by invoice after a credit check, due 30 days after the invoice date.

## Large orders

For business orders over 5,000 dollars, you need a written quote from the trade desk before placing the order.

## Returns for trade customers

The returns policy applies. Returns of 10 or more units of the same item need the trade desk's approval.
