# RAG: Building AI That Uses Your Data

Files for the course [RAG: Building AI That Uses Your Data](https://learning.nextia-ai.com/courses/rag/).

| Folder | What it has |
|---|---|
| [`corpus/`](corpus) | Larkfield's policy collection: 36 synthetic documents (Markdown, HTML and PDF), the inventory, four documents in all three formats, a [dataset card](corpus/dataset.md) and the [list of deliberate difficulties](corpus/difficulties.md). CC0. |
| [`questions/`](questions) | Grace's 67 questions with reference answers and relevant passages. CC0. |
| [`snapshots/`](snapshots) | The course project `policy-assistant`: `start` (download it in the lesson *Corpus and questions*) and the project at the end of each module. Code MIT; documents, questions and recordings CC0. |

You need no account and no key. Search runs on your computer with SQLite and two small open models. Answers come from recorded model responses; a live model is optional (a free local model through Ollama, or your own key).
