# Gift cards

## Buying a gift card
We sell gift cards from 25 to 500 dollars. You cannot buy a gift card with another gift card.

## Using a gift card
Gift cards never expire and have no fees. You can check the balance in your account.

## Lost cards and returns
We can replace a lost gift card if you give us the order number of the purchase.

Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it.
