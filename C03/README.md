# Essential Mathematics and Statistics for AI

Optional notebooks for [Essential Mathematics and Statistics for AI](https://learning.nextia-ai.com/courses/math/).

The course needs no code: every lesson works with a calculator and the explorers on its page. Each notebook here lets you check a lesson's numbers and redo its tasks in Python, with checks that tell you when your answer is right. Each folder `Mnn-Lnn-<lesson>/` has the notebook and its solution. They use Python's standard library (one lesson also uses numpy) and open in Colab, Kaggle or Jupyter.

*Mathematical reading skills* (Module 1, lesson 1) has no notebook: its calculations are one step each.
