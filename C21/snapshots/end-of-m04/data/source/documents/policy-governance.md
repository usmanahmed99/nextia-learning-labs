# How Larkfield policies are managed

## Purpose

This collection is the single source of Larkfield's customer policies, staff procedures, product guides, help articles and notices.

Our help-desk agents and the policy assistant answer only from these documents.

## Document types

- **Policy:** The rules for customers, covering returns, refunds and payments, delivery, warranty, privacy, Rewards, and others. Owned by the team named in the document.
- **Procedure:** Step-by-step instructions for help-desk staff. Always labelled staff.
- **Product guide:** How to use and care for a product, its parts and part numbers.
- **Help article:** Short answers for customers, such as the help centre FAQ.
- **Notice:** A time-limited document, such as a safety notice or a holiday change.
- **Supplier content:** Text written by a supplier about its products, published as received. It is not checked by Larkfield.

## Versions and effective dates

Every document has a version number and an effective-from date. Some also have an effective-to date.

A new version replaces the old one from its effective-from date. The old version stays in the archive. A version can be published before its effective-from date; until that date, the previous version still applies.

Which version applies depends on the date of the event:
- For returns, the version in force on the date the order was delivered.
- For other questions, the version in force on the day of the question.

## When documents disagree

- **Rule 1:** A safety notice wins over every other document.
- **Rule 2:** A policy wins over a product guide, a help article and supplier content.
- **Rule 3:** Supplier content never changes a Larkfield policy. For example, the warranty on a product is the one in the Larkfield warranty policy, whatever the supplier's text says.
- **Rule 4:** Between two documents of the same type, the one with the later effective-from date wins.

Agents who find a disagreement report it to Grace, the support lead, who owns this document.

## Access labels

- **public:** The document may be shown or quoted to customers.
- **staff:** Only for help-desk staff. Never quote or send it to a customer.

## Languages

Most documents are in English. Some documents for customers in Quebec are written in French.

A French document has the same authority as an English document of the same type.

Agents answer customers in English or French, in the customer's language.
