# Ember GR-310 gas BBQ: care and safety

## Fuel

The GR-310 runs on propane from a standard 9 kg cylinder.

The natural gas conversion kit, NG-310, is sold separately. Only a licensed gas fitter may install it.

## Leak test at the start of each season

Brush soapy water on the hose and connections with the cylinder valve open and the burners off.

Bubbles mean a leak: close the cylinder and do not use the BBQ.

## If you smell gas

Turn off the burners and close the cylinder. Do not light the BBQ. Contact us at once.

## Cleaning

Burn off the grill for 15 minutes after cooking, then brush the grates.

Empty the grease tray after every use.

## Parts

- Replacement stainless-steel burner: BR-310-2 (three are needed for the GR-310).
- Fitted cover: CV-310.
