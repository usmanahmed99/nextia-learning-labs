# Caring for garden furniture

## Teak and other hardwoods
Clean with mild soapy water. Apply teak oil twice a year, in spring and in autumn.

## Aluminium and steel
Wash with mild soap and water. Do not use abrasive pads.

## Cushions and covers
Store cushions indoors when they are not used. Most covers can be washed at 30 °C.

## Winter
Store furniture under cover or indoors from November to March.

## Warranty
Your garden furniture has a 2-year warranty against defects. Cushions and fabric covers have a 1-year warranty.

Rust from leaving steel furniture uncovered in winter is not covered.
