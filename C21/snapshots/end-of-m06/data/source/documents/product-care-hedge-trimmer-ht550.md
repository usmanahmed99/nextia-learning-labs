# HT-550 corded hedge trimmer: care and use

## Specifications
- Motor: 550 W.
- Blade length: 55 cm.
- Weight: 3.4 kg.

## Using it safely
Plug it into an outdoor outlet with GFCI protection. Do not use it in the rain or on wet hedges.

Keep the cable behind you, away from the blade.

## After each use
Unplug it, clean the blade, and put a few drops of blade oil LB-1 along the blade.

Fit the blade guard before you store it.

## Warranty
Corded power tools have a 3-year warranty. Blades are a consumable part and are not covered.
