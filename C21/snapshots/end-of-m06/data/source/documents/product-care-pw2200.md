# PW-2200 electric pressure washer: care and parts

## Specifications

- Maximum pressure: 2,200 PSI.
- Water flow: 6.4 litres per minute.
- Electric motor: 1,800 W. Plug into an outdoor outlet with a GFCI (ground-fault) protection.
- High-pressure hose: 8 m.

## Before each use

Connect the garden hose and turn the water on fully before you start the motor.

Check the hose and the trigger gun for leaks.

## Using it safely

Never run the pressure washer for more than 2 minutes with the trigger released: the pump overheats.

Never point the spray at people, animals or electrical outlets.

Use only detergents sold for pressure washers.

## Winter storage

Drain all water from the pump and hoses before temperatures fall below 0 °C.

Store it indoors, above 0 °C. Frost damage is not covered by the warranty.

## Troubleshooting

- Pressure pulses up and down: clean the inlet filter and check that the water tap is fully open.
- Motor hums but does not start: release the trigger, wait 2 minutes, try again.
