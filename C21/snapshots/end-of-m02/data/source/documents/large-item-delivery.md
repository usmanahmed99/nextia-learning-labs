# Large item delivery

## What counts as a large item

A large item is an item over 30 kg or longer than 2 metres. Examples include sheds, greenhouses, BBQs, patio sets and trampolines.

## Delivery options and fees

- Curbside delivery: 39 dollars. The free-shipping threshold does not apply to large items.
- Room-of-choice delivery by a two-person team: 79 dollars.
- Haul-away of an old item of the same kind: 25 dollars, only with room-of-choice delivery.

We deliver large items in the provinces only, not in the territories.

Free standard shipping for Larkfield Rewards Gold members does not cover large item delivery fees.

## Booking the delivery

The courier calls you within 2 business days after the item ships to book a 4-hour delivery window. Deliveries take place Monday to Saturday.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is there, the first new booking is free. Each further missed delivery costs 39 dollars.

## Assembly

Delivery does not include assembly. See assembly and installation services.
