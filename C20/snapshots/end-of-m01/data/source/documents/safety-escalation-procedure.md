# Safety escalation procedure

## When to use it

Use this procedure for fire, smoke, a burning smell, sparks, overheating, an electric shock, water near electricity, a gas smell, or any injury—even if the customer raises it only as a worry.

## What to tell the customer

Tell the customer:
- Stop using the product and unplug it, or close the gas cylinder.
- Do not try to repair it.
- Keep the product and its packaging; do not throw them away.

## What you do

- Mark the ticket as needing a person. Do not troubleshoot.
- Write down the product, the lot number or serial number, and what happened, in the customer's words.

The product safety team contacts the customer within 1 business hour.

If someone was injured, product safety reports the incident to Health Canada within 2 days.

## What you never write

Never admit fault and never promise compensation in writing. Product safety decides.
