# Help centre: frequently asked questions

## Where is my order?
Open Orders in your account to see the status and the tracking link. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in the provinces.

## How do I return an item?
Go to Orders in your account, choose the item and select Return an item. Or contact the help desk.

We email a return label within 1 business day. Drop the parcel at any post office. Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## How long does a refund take?
Refunds reach your card within 3 business days after we receive your return.

## Do you match other shops' prices?
No. If we lower our own price within 14 days after your delivery, you can ask for the difference.

## Can I change my delivery address?
Yes, while the order status is Processing. After the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## I forgot my password
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. We never ask for your password by email, chat or phone.

## Do you deliver to the territories?
Yes, to Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut, in 7 to 12 business days. Large items cannot be delivered there.
