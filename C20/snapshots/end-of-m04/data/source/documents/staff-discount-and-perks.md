# Staff discount and perks

## Staff discount

You get 20% off all products, except gift cards. The discount applies to at most 2,000 dollars of purchases in a calendar year.

You cannot combine the staff discount with other promotions or discount codes.

## Rules

Your staff code is personal. Never share it with customers, friends or family.

Misuse of the staff discount is a disciplinary matter.
