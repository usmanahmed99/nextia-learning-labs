# AquaFlow hose reels: product information

## The AquaFlow range
- HR-30: wall-mounted hose reel with 30 m of hose.
- HR-50: hose reel cart on wheels with 50 m of hose.
- Both have a 10-position spray nozzle and brass connectors.

## Installation
Fix the HR-30 to a wall with the 4 screws supplied, at least 1 m above the ground. Connect the short lead-in hose to the tap.

## Care
Drain the hose and bring the reel indoors before the first frost. Do not leave the hose under pressure when you are not using it.

## Support

For help with your AquaFlow reel, contact the shop where you bought it.
