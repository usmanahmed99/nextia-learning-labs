# Warranty claims procedure

## Before you start

This procedure is for the warranty team. Do not share it with customers.

If the customer mentions fire, smoke, a burning smell, sparks, an electric shock, a gas smell or an injury, stop and follow the safety escalation procedure instead.

## Check the claim

- Find the order and its delivery date.
- Find the product category and its period in the table of the warranty policy. Check that the claim is inside the period.
- Ask for a photo or short video. For power tools and pressure washers, ask for the serial number.

## Approval limits

- An agent may approve a repair, replacement or refund worth up to 300 dollars.
- From 300 dollars up to 1,000 dollars, a team lead must approve.
- Over 1,000 dollars, the support lead (Grace) must approve.

## Open the case

Create a warranty case named WAR- followed by the order number, for example WAR-LK-581106.

When the product must come back, create a return authorisation numbered RMA-YYMMDD-NNN (the date and a 3-digit counter), for example RMA-261009-004.

## Faulty product: return or not

- Products worth 75 dollars or more: send a free return label; we claim from the supplier.
- Products under 75 dollars: the customer does not return them; ask them to recycle the product responsibly.

## Pressure washer pumps

- If the pump of a PW-2200 fails within 3 years, send the pump kit PK-2200-07 instead of a new pressure washer.
- For the PW-2400, send the pump kit PK-2400-03.
- Offer a technician visit only if the customer cannot fit the kit.
