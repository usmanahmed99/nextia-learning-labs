# Safety notice: KT-180 electric kettle, lots 2607 and 2608

## Which kettles are affected

Larkfield KT-180 electric kettles with lot number 2607 or 2608 are affected. The lot number is printed under the base.

KT-180 kettles from lot 2609 onwards, and all other kettle models, are not affected.

## The risk

The kettle may not switch off when the water boils and can overheat.

## What to do

- Stop using the kettle at once and unplug it.
- Contact the help desk with your order number and the lot number.

## What we offer

We offer a full refund, or a free KT-185 kettle as a replacement. This offer applies even if the return window or the warranty has ended.

Do not send the kettle back by post. Take it to an electronics recycling point.
