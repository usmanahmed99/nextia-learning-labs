# Courier claims for damaged or lost parcels

## Purpose
We file claims with the courier when a parcel is damaged or lost. The customer never deals with the courier.

## File the claim
- File the claim in the courier's portal within 7 days after the customer reports the problem.
- Attach the customer's photos and the tracking number.
- Name the claim CLM- followed by the courier code and the order number, for example CLM-PUR-LK-702219.

## Courier codes
- CPX: Canada Post Expedited, for parcels.
- PUR: Purolator, for express parcels.
- LTL: the freight carrier for large items.

## While the claim is open
Do not wait for the courier's answer before sending the customer a replacement or a refund.
