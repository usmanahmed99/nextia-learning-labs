# Live plants: delivery and guarantee

## When we ship plants
We ship live plants from April to October only.

## The plant guarantee
If a perennial, shrub or tree dies within 1 year of delivery, we replace it once or give you store credit.

Annuals and houseplants are guaranteed for 30 days after delivery.

Send us a photo of the plant and your order number.

## Returns
Live plants cannot be returned because you changed your mind. The guarantee above applies instead.
