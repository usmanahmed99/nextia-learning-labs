# Your account and security

## Passwords

We never ask for your password or your full card number, by email, chat or phone.

To reset your password, use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. The link is valid for 30 minutes.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Two-step verification

You can turn on two-step verification in your account settings. It is optional.

## Changing your email address

We send a confirmation link to your old email address. The change happens only after you confirm it.

## Saved cards

Saved cards are stored by our payment processor. Your account shows only the last 4 digits.

## Deleting your account

Ask for deletion with the form in your account settings. We delete the account within 30 days.

## Guest checkout

You can order without an account. Guest orders cannot earn Rewards points.
