# Refunds and payments policy

## Accepted payment methods
We accept:
- Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards.
- Visa Debit and Mastercard Debit.
- PayPal.
- Larkfield gift cards.

We do not accept cheques or cash on delivery.

## When we charge you
Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in several boxes, each box is charged when it ships.

## How long a refund takes
When a refund is approved, we send it to the original payment method within 2 business days. The money then usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

PayPal refunds usually appear within 5 business days. Refunds to a gift card appear at once.

## Double charges
If you were charged twice for the same order, we refund the extra charge within 3 business days after we confirm it.

## Store credit
Instead of a refund, you can choose store credit. Store credit is issued at once and never expires.

## Price adjustments
If we lower the price of an item within 14 days after your delivery, you can ask for the difference once per item.

We do not match the prices of other shops.

## Prices, currency and taxes
All prices are in Canadian dollars. Sales taxes (GST, HST, PST or QST) are charged according to the delivery province.

## Declined payments
If a payment is declined, we hold the order for 48 hours. If it is not paid by then, the order is cancelled.
