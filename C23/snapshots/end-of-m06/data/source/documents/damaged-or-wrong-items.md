# Damaged, faulty on arrival or wrong items

## Report within 14 days

Report an item that arrived damaged, faulty or wrong within 14 days of delivery. Send a photo of the item and of the label on the box.

## Replacement or refund

We send a replacement at no cost, or a full refund if no replacement is in stock.

## Do I need to send it back?

- Items under 50 dollars: no, you can keep or recycle the damaged item.
- Items of 50 dollars or more: we send a free return label, or arrange a free pickup for items over 30 kg.

## Wrong item

We send a free return label. We ship the right item as soon as the courier scans the return parcel.

## Missing parts

Tell us the part number from the manual. We send missing parts free of charge within 5 business days.

## After 14 days

A fault found more than 14 days after delivery is handled under the warranty policy.
