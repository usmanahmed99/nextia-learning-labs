# Recycling and take-back

## Batteries
Send used Larkfield Volt batteries back to us with a free label from the help desk. We recycle them.

## Old appliances and furniture
With room-of-choice delivery of a large item, we can take away an old item of the same kind for 25 dollars.

## Packaging
Our boxes and paper padding can go in household recycling.

## What we cannot take back
We cannot take back garden chemicals, paint, fuel and propane cylinders. Take them to a municipal hazardous waste depot.
