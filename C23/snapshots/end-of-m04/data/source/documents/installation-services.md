# Assembly and installation services

## Where the service is available

Assembly and installation services are available in Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal, Vancouver and Calgary, through a partner company.

## Prices

- Shed assembly, up to 10 square metres: 199 dollars.
- Patio set assembly: 79 dollars.
- BBQ assembly: 59 dollars.

## Booking

You can book from the order page within 30 days after delivery.

You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before the appointment.

## What is not included

- Gas connections and natural gas conversions: these need a licensed gas fitter.
- Concrete bases for sheds.
