# Delivery policy

## Where we deliver

We deliver to every province and territory in Canada. We do not deliver outside Canada.

Items over 30 kg are not delivered to post office boxes. Large items follow the large item delivery policy and are not delivered to the territories.

## Delivery times and fees

Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, except statutory holidays) from the day your order ships. The free-shipping threshold is the order value before tax.

| Destination | Standard delivery | Standard fee | Express delivery (1 to 2 business days, 14.95 dollars) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario and Quebec | 3 to 5 business days | 9.95 dollars, free at 100 dollars or more | Toronto, Ottawa and Montreal |
| British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba | 3 to 5 business days | 9.95 dollars, free at 100 dollars or more | Vancouver, Calgary and Edmonton |
| New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador | 3 to 5 business days | 9.95 dollars, free at 100 dollars or more | Not available |
| Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut | 7 to 12 business days | 9.95 dollars, free at 100 dollars or more, plus a 15.00-dollar northern surcharge on every order | Not available |

Express delivery is not available for large items.

## Late and lost orders

If your order arrives more than 5 business days late, we refund the shipping fee.

An order more than 15 business days late is treated as lost. We send a replacement or a full refund, as you prefer.

## Changing the address or the delivery date

You can change the address or delivery date while your order status is Processing.

Once your order has shipped, we cannot change the address or redirect the parcel.

## Orders in several boxes

Some orders ship in several boxes that can arrive on different days.

If a box is missing, wait 2 business days after the first box arrives, then contact us.

## Signature on delivery

Orders over 500 dollars need a signature on delivery.

## Damaged parcels

If an item arrives damaged, follow the policy for damaged, faulty or wrong items: send a photo within 14 days of delivery.
