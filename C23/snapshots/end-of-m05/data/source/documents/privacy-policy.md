# Privacy policy

## What we collect

We collect your name, addresses, email, phone number, orders, and help-desk messages. Our payment processor holds your payment details; we see only the last 4 digits of a card.

## Why we use it

We use your information to deliver orders, answer your questions, prevent fraud, and send marketing emails if you agreed to them.

## How long we keep it

- Orders and invoices: 7 years, as tax law requires.
- Help-desk tickets and chat transcripts: 24 months after the ticket is closed.
- Phone call recordings: 90 days.
- Marketing preferences: until you change them.

## Who we share it with

We share your information with couriers, our payment processor and our help-desk software provider, only as needed to serve you.

We do not sell personal information. Your data is stored in Canada.

## Our help-desk assistant

Our help-desk staff use an assistant that drafts answers from Larkfield's policy documents. Your messages are not used to train AI models.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal information. We answer within 30 days.

You can ask us to correct your personal information, or to delete your account. We delete the account within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

## Contact

Write to our privacy office at privacy@larkfield.example.
