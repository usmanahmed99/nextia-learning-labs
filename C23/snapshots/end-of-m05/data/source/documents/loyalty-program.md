# Larkfield Rewards

## Joining
Larkfield Rewards is free. Join in your account.

## Earning points
You earn 1 point for every dollar spent, before tax and after discounts. Gift card purchases do not earn points.

## Using points
200 points give you a 10-dollar reward to use at checkout. Points cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another person.

## Expiry
Points expire after 18 months without any purchase.

## Gold tier
Members who earn 1,000 points or more in a calendar year become Gold for the next calendar year.

Gold members get free standard shipping on every order and early access to sales.

## Returns and points
When you return an item, we remove the points it earned.
